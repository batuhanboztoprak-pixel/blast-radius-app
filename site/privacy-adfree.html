Blast Radius
Privacy Policy

Effective date: [date 1.0.1 goes live]

Blast Radius (“we”, “us”) is an asteroid impact simulator for iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. The short version: we don’t run any servers, we don’t have accounts, and we never receive your personal data. The only third parties involved are Apple (maps, purchases) and NASA/JPL (public asteroid data). There are no ads and no tracking.

Information we collect

None. Blast Radius has no backend, no sign-in and no analytics of its own. We can’t see who uses the app or what you simulate.

Information stored on your device

The app saves a few things locally so they survive a restart: your units (km or miles), whether you’ve unlocked Pro, the running “world population left” counter, which free tries you’ve used, and similar settings. They stay on your iPhone and are deleted when you delete the app.

Location

If you tap the “use my location” button, iOS asks for permission and the app reads your position once to drop the impact pin there. Your location is used only on your device and is never sent to us. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

Maps and place search

Maps, place search and address lookups are provided by Apple (MapKit). When you search for a place, the search text is sent to Apple to find it. Apple’s handling of this data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Real asteroid data

To show “today’s real asteroid”, the app downloads the public list of upcoming close approaches from NASA/JPL’s Close-Approach Data service (ssd-api.jpl.nasa.gov) about twice a day. It’s a plain public request: nothing about you, your location or your simulations is sent, and the result is cached on your device. Like any web request, it reveals your IP address to NASA’s servers.

No ads, no tracking

Blast Radius shows no ads and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. It never asks to track you across other apps and websites, and it doesn’t use your device’s advertising identifier.

Purchases

Blast Radius Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details or Apple ID. Apple tells the app only whether Pro has been purchased, so it can unlock it and restore it on your other devices.

Children

Blast Radius is not directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect information from children.

Your rights

Because we don’t hold any personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data processed by Apple, use the controls and contacts in Apple’s privacy policy. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU or KVKK in Türkiye), you may have additional rights; contact us and we’ll help where we can.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the effective date above.
